Australia's Climate

How Australia has been impacted by climate change

Australia has been impacted by climate change in several ways. Primarily, Australia is impacted by climate change through increased and more intense wildfires and the deterioration of coral reefs due to the ocean climate changing. These factors are caused by temperature changes and droughts changing the environment of the country.

How Australia will be impacted by climate change

Australia will face more intense wildfires as climate change continues. Climate change will also lead to sea level rise, which is dangerous as most majr Australian cities are on or near the coast. Australia will also face crop shortages as droughts and heatwaves reduce crop yield.

What can be done to reduce the effects of climate change

  1. reducing emissions across the country
  2. using renewable energy
  3. reduce carbon emissions from mining and industry

What is being done to reduce the effects of climate change

  1. investment in renewable energy
  2. risk assesment through climate service mapping
  3. a net-zero plan to cut emissions
